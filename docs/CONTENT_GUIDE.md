# Content and Brand Guide — Nayana Villa

- **Version:** 1.0
- **Brand:** Nayana Villa (fictional)
- **Tagline:** Where Nature Meets Serenity.
- **Languages:** English and Bahasa Indonesia

---

## 1. Brand Identity

Nayana Villa is a fictional boutique luxury villa concept inspired by tropical landscapes and thoughtful architecture.

### Brand attributes
- Calm.
- Warm.
- Natural.
- Refined.
- Welcoming.
- Considered.

### Brand promise
A peaceful, thoughtfully presented retreat concept where nature and comfort come together.

### Tagline
**Where Nature Meets Serenity.**

Do not imply that the fictional villa is a real operating property.

## 2. Target Audience

### International travelers
Use clear, natural English. Avoid overly ornate language and unexplained local expressions.

### Domestic travelers
Use warm, clear Bahasa Indonesia. Avoid stiff or overly formal phrasing.

### Property owners evaluating the portfolio
The content should demonstrate how a hospitality brand can communicate its offering professionally and clearly.

## 3. Tone of Voice

### Do
- Be warm and calm.
- Use concise, sensory language.
- Focus on the experience and practical information.
- Keep claims specific and understandable.
- Use consistent terminology across pages.

### Avoid
- Exaggerated luxury claims.
- Unsupported superlatives.
- Fake awards or ratings.
- Fake testimonials.
- False urgency.
- Guaranteed booking or availability claims.
- Generic filler copy.

## 4. Sample Copy

### English hero
**A Sanctuary in the Heart of Nature.**

Discover a peaceful retreat where tropical landscapes, thoughtful architecture, and warm hospitality come together to create a memorable stay.

CTA: **Explore Our Villa**

### Indonesian hero
**Temukan Ketenangan di Tengah Alam.**

Jelajahi konsep tempat beristirahat yang tenang, tempat lanskap tropis, arsitektur yang dirancang dengan penuh perhatian, dan keramahan hangat berpadu dalam satu pengalaman.

CTA: **Jelajahi Villa Kami**

These are sample demonstration copy, not factual claims about a real property.

## 6. Fictional Property Data

The following may be used as illustrative content only:
- Property name: Nayana Villa.
- General area: Ubud, Bali.
- Property category: Boutique luxury villa.
- Accommodation names: Garden Suite, Canopy Villa, Nayana Residence.
- Facilities: illustrative amenities selected for the demo.
- Guest capacities, room counts, areas, and indicative prices: simulated values.

Do not invent an exact street address, coordinates, operating license, real availability, or real business contact details.

Any illustrative rates must be labeled clearly as demo content or replaced with “Contact for rates.”

## 7. Testimonials, Ratings, Awards, and Claims

Do not publish fabricated:
- Guest testimonials.
- Guest names or photographs.
- Star ratings.
- Review counts.
- Awards.
- Certifications.
- Press mentions.
- Occupancy statistics.
- Booking conversion statistics.

If a design needs a testimonial layout, use a clearly labeled placeholder in development only and do not publish it as real evidence.

## 8. Image and Asset Policy

Use only:
- Original images created for the project.
- Images generated for the project where permitted.
- Properly licensed stock images.
- Images with explicit permission for use.

For each image, record:
- Local filename.
- Source or creator.
- License or permission basis.
- Required attribution.
- Date obtained.

## 9. Localization Quality

- Translate meaning, not word-for-word structure.
- Keep CTA meaning consistent.
- Keep dates, currencies, and number formatting appropriate to locale.
- Avoid mixed-language labels.
- Review all validation and error messages.
- Keep brand names unchanged.
- Ensure language switching does not silently lose user-entered form data.

## 10. Privacy Copy for Inquiry Form

Suggested English:
"By continuing, you agree that the information you provide may be used to respond to your inquiry. This demo form prepares a message for WhatsApp and does not create a confirmed reservation."

Suggested Indonesian:
"Dengan melanjutkan, Anda menyetujui penggunaan informasi yang diberikan untuk menanggapi permintaan Anda. Formulir demo ini menyiapkan pesan WhatsApp dan tidak membuat reservasi yang telah dikonfirmasi."

For a real client project, replace this with a privacy notice reviewed for the actual business and applicable laws.
